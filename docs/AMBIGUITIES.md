# Ambiguities & Edge-Case Decisions — VorldX Employee Attendance System

This document catalogs every ambiguous, under-specified, or potentially conflicting requirement identified between the **Project Brief (`docs/assignment.pdf`)**, enterprise attendance domain rules, and system architecture.

Per project instructions:

- Where the PDF and any document disagree, **the PDF is strictly followed**.
- Every ambiguity has a proposed engineering default.
- Every entry specifies whether the behavior must be configurable by an administrator.

---

## 1. Ambiguity & Conflict Register

| ID         | Topic                                           | Ambiguity / Conflict                                                                                                                                                                                           | Proposed Default Resolution                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Must Be Configurable?                                                                                               | Authority / Discrepancy Note                                                                                                                                    |

| **AMB-01** | **QR Token Rotation Interval**                  | The PDF states QR codes are "short-lived and rotate periodically" without prescribing an exact duration in seconds.                                                                                            | Rotate QR cryptographic token every **20 seconds**. Server accepts tokens within a **30-second window** (current token + 10s grace for network latency).                                                                                                                                                                        | **Yes** (Configurable in Admin Policy settings between 10s and 60s).                                                | Inferred specification consistent with PDF Section 3.                                                                                                           |
| **AMB-02** | **Punch Toggle vs Explicit Intent**             | PDF states "Employees check in and check out by scanning the dynamic QR code". It does not specify if the scan automatically determines direction or requires employee button selection.                       | **Intelligent Automatic Toggle**: First valid scan of the calendar day records **Check-In**. Subsequent scan records **Check-Out** and computes active work duration. If an employee scans a 3rd time, it updates their check-out timestamp with confirmation prompt.                                                           | **Yes** (Configurable: `Single check-in with rolling check-out` vs `Strict two-punch per day`).                     | PDF Section 3 dictates both check-in and check-out occur via QR scan; auto-toggle provides the cleanest mobile UX.                                              |
| **AMB-03** | **Desktop Evaluator QR Scanning Demonstration** | PDF requires an end-to-end demonstrable flow for both mobile app and QR display. In local development or evaluator environments without dual physical devices or webcams, camera scanning could block testing. | **Dual Scan Modes in Mobile App**: (1) Live camera viewfinder using WebRTC for real camera devices, plus (2) **"Simulate Scan / Quick Punch"** button that fetches the active terminal's current valid token for 1-click evaluation on a single computer screen.                                                                | **No** (Both modes available seamlessly in demonstration environment).                                              | Satisfies PDF Section 3 & 4 requirement for complete end-to-end demonstration.                                                                                  |
| **AMB-04** | **Simulated OTP Delivery & Display**            | PDF mandates "first-time account verification can be demonstrated with a simulated OTP flow" without real email delivery. Where is the OTP surfaced to the user?                                               | **Inline Demo OTP Notification**: When the user inputs their assigned Employee ID, the system generates a 6-digit OTP and renders an interactive helper alert banner directly above the input: `[DEMO HELPER] OTP sent to employee profile: 123456`. An auto-fill button allows 1-click verification.                           | **Yes** (Toggleable: helper banner visible in Demo mode; can accept master code `123456`).                          | Follows PDF Section 3 ("The demo verification flow should show how account verification works without requiring real employee records or real email delivery"). |
| **AMB-05** | **Late Arrival Threshold & Grace Period**       | PDF states "Late attendance is determined using the configured work schedule and grace period", but does not provide default hours.                                                                            | **Standard Schedule**: Default shift starts at `09:00 AM` with a **15-minute grace period** (`09:15 AM`). Scans before 09:15 AM are marked `PRESENT`. Scans at 09:16 AM or later are marked `LATE`.                                                                                                                             | **Yes** (Admin can configure shift start time, end time, and grace period in minutes).                              | Strictly follows PDF Section 3.                                                                                                                                 |
| **AMB-06** | **Attendance Percentage Calculation Formula**   | PDF states employee views "attendance percentage", but does not specify how weekends, holidays, and WFH days impact the denominator.                                                                           | Formula: `Attendance % = ((Present Days + Approved WFH Days + Half Days * 0.5) / Total Working Days Elapsed in Month) * 100`. Saturdays and Sundays (or configured non-working days) are excluded from `Total Working Days`.                                                                                                    | **Yes** (Configurable: whether Late days receive 100% attendance credit or a penalty, and working days definition). | Inferred domain standard aligned with PDF Section 3.                                                                                                            |
| **AMB-07** | **Approved WFH Impact on Daily Attendance**     | PDF specifies that employees submit WFH requests and HR approves/rejects them. Does approval automatically mark attendance, or must employee still punch remotely?                                             | **Automatic Attendance Credit**: When HR approves a WFH request for Date $D$, the system automatically creates or updates the `attendance_records` row for Date $D$ with status `WFH_APPROVED`. The employee does not need to scan the office QR code.                                                                          | **Yes** (Toggle: `Auto-create WFH attendance on approval` vs `Require remote confirmation punch`).                  | PDF Section 6 explicitly excludes GPS/location-based tracking, so remote geofence punches are prohibited. Auto-credit is the compliant solution.                |
| **AMB-08** | **WFH Monthly Quota and Eligibility Rules**     | PDF states "Employees can view WFH eligibility and submit a request where permitted." The criteria for permission are not defined.                                                                             | **Policy-Driven Quota**: Each employee profile or department has: (1) `wfh_allowed` (Boolean), and (2) `monthly_wfh_quota` (Default: 4 days/month). If quota is exhausted or role is marked ineligible, request submission is disabled with clear user feedback.                                                                | **Yes** (Admin can edit department and employee WFH eligibility and quotas in the Policy manager).                  | Aligns with PDF Section 3 ("Employees can view WFH eligibility... where permitted").                                                                            |
| **AMB-09** | **Permitted Corrections & Audit Trail Scope**   | PDF notes HR can "View, filter, and manage attendance records, including permitted corrections." Does not specify what fields are editable or who can edit.                                                    | **HR Admin Only with Mandatory Reason**: Only authenticated Admin/HR can correct punch times or change status (`PRESENT`, `LATE`, `WFH`, `ABSENT`). Every modification requires a non-empty `reason` field and writes an immutable row into `audit_logs`.                                                                       | **No** (Audit logging and mandatory justification are permanent non-bypassable security controls).                  | Satisfies PDF Section 3 ("permitted corrections" and "audit records").                                                                                          |
| **AMB-10** | **Office QR Display Architectural Identity**    | PDF Section 2 & 8 emphasizes: "The office QR display is a dedicated office screen showing a dynamic QR code... It is a supporting system component, not a third application."                                  | The QR Display is implemented as a dedicated `/terminal/display` route inside the application, paired to the backend via a unique `Terminal ID` and pairing secret. It operates in fullscreen kiosk mode without requiring employee login.                                                                                      | **Yes** (Admin can create, rename, pair, unpair, and toggle active status of multiple terminals).                   | Directly adheres to PDF Section 1, 2, and 8.                                                                                                                    |
| **AMB-11** | **Half-Day vs Incomplete Duration Rules**       | PDF mentions tracking "duration where available" but does not define minimum working hour thresholds.                                                                                                          | Thresholds: (1) `Full Day`: $\ge 8$ hours, (2) `Half Day`: $\ge 4$ hours and $< 8$ hours, (3) `Incomplete`: $< 4$ hours. Status badge indicates whether day was full or half day.                                                                                                                                               | **Yes** (Configurable: `min_full_day_hours` default 8, `min_half_day_hours` default 4).                             | Inferred logic necessary to give meaningful semantic value to "duration".                                                                                       |
| **AMB-12** | **Replay Protection on QR Tokens**              | PDF requires rotating QR codes. It does not state whether a single dynamic token can be scanned by 20 employees standing in line during its 20-second validity window.                                         | **Shared Valid Window Token**: An office QR display shows a token valid for 20 seconds for the entire office entrance line. Multiple distinct employees CAN check in using that valid token during that 20s window. However: an individual employee CANNOT check in twice with the same token, and expired tokens are rejected. | **Yes** (Window length configurable).                                                                               | Standard real-world office terminal behavior.                                                                                                                   |
